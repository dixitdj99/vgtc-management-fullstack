Official Privacy Policy • Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Vikas Goods Transport Co ("VGTC", "we", "us", or "our") operates freight and transport management services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you interact with our management systems, website (http://vgtc.site), and automated WhatsApp messaging services powered by Meta Cloud API.

2. Information We Collect

To provide logistics and transport tracking services, we collect relevant business and operational information, including:

  • Contact Information: Phone numbers, names of truck owners, drivers, clerks, and logistics partners.
  • Operational Data: Vehicle registration numbers, Loading Receipts (LR), Freight Vouchers, transport routes, goods weight, and trip details.
  • WhatsApp Communications: Delivery receipts, verification alerts, and trip status updates sent via Meta WhatsApp Cloud API.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for transport management operations:

  • Dispatching real-time Loading Receipts (LR) and dispatch slips to vehicle owners and drivers.
  • Sending payment receipts, freight balance updates, and operational alerts.
  • Facilitating two-way communication for loading verification and advance approvals.
  • Ensuring compliance with transportation record-keeping regulations.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share data with authorized infrastructure providers strictly necessary to deliver our services:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc. (WhatsApp Cloud API): Message templates and phone numbers are processed by Meta to deliver authorized WhatsApp notifications.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption and administrative safeguards to protect your personal and operational information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

6. User Rights and Opt-Out

Recipients of automated WhatsApp notifications can opt out at any time by replying "STOP" or contacting our support team directly. Truck owners and drivers can request access, correction, or deletion of their contact records.
